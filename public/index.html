Why a second passkey?

Lab plan has no email and no password reset. Sign-in is only your username plus a passkey. If every passkey for this account is gone, the account is gone with it — we can’t email you a recovery link.

A passkey lives on a device (or in a password manager). Lose that phone, wipe that laptop, or clear the keychain without a backup, and you’re locked out unless another passkey still works.

Best practice: enroll two different devices — for example a laptop and a phone. Then one broken or lost device doesn’t strand the lab notebook. You can also mint a one-time enroll code in Settings for a device that isn’t signed in yet.

More detail: How passkeys work.

Already covered by a cloud sync?

Some passkeys sync across devices through a password manager (iCloud Passwords, Google Password Manager, Proton Pass, Bitwarden, 1Password, and similar). If that is how your only key works — unlockable on more than one device — you can hide this warning. A single hardware-only key on one phone is not enough.
